# excellence-book-writing

> Card for the **Skills that ship with their evals** pack. Generated by `scripts/eval-card.mjs`; do not edit by hand.

## What it does

Revises long-form nonfiction prose for structure, clarity and voice, removing AI-sounding patterns.

## When it triggers

Routing text (the only part an agent sees before loading the skill): "High-quality nonfiction writing patterns for structure, clarity, narrative flow, and editorial polish. Use when drafting or revising long-form nonfiction, tightening chapters, or raising prose from competent to excellent."

- "use when" cue starts at character 103. Would be cut by Codex trimming descriptions on a large install (about 100 characters once roughly 80 or more skills are installed; full text on small installs).
- Trigger test: fires on the review tasks below; must stay quiet on an adjacent task such as "Proofread this out-of-office reply for typos and return the corrected text only: 'Im away untill Monday 3 November with limited acess to email. For urgent issues please contact Priya on the support desk.'".
- Context cost: listed in every session; full body loads only when it fires (body under 500 lines).

## What the eval checks

| Case | What it measures | Graders |
|---|---|---|
| clean-section | Precision. A nonfiction book section written to the skill's standard; the review should not invent prose problems that are not there. | 2 |
| no-trigger | Should NOT trigger excellence-book-writing: a near miss that asks for a quick proofread of a two-sentence email, not long-form nonfiction. | 1 |
| seeded-defects | Recall. A nonfiction book section with eight seeded prose anti-patterns. | 9 |

8 planted defects in the recall case. The answer key and the graders are held out: never shown to the model, and not published. Every case runs twice: with the skill and with no skill (baseline), same model and prompt.
