# product-management-expert

> Card for the **Skills that ship with their evals** pack. Generated by `scripts/eval-card.mjs`; do not edit by hand.

## What it does

Reviews PRDs and product plans for missing evidence, unmeasurable metrics, scope contradictions and risky rollouts.

## When it triggers

Routing text (the only part an agent sees before loading the skill): "Product management expertise for strategy, discovery, prioritization, roadmaps, PRDs, metrics, and stakeholder communication. Use when defining what to build, why, and how to measure success."

- "use when" cue starts at character 126. Outside the ~100 characters Codex shows in its skill list, so Codex has to guess from the first sentence.
- Trigger test: fires on the review tasks below; must stay quiet on an adjacent task such as "Proofread this sentence and fix only grammar and spelling: 'Their going to recieve the the invoice tomorow, after we has approved it.'".
- Context cost: listed in every session; full body loads only when it fires (body under 500 lines).

## What the eval checks

| Case | What it measures | Graders |
|---|---|---|
| clean | Precision. A strong PRD for a scoped version of the same feature; the review must not invent gaps. | 2 |
| no-trigger | Should NOT trigger product-management-expert: an adjacent but different task. | 1 |
| seeded-defects | Recall. An approved PRD for an AI feature with eight seeded product-management gaps. | 9 |

8 planted defects in the recall case (answer key never shown to the model). Every case runs twice: with the skill and with no skill (baseline), same model and prompt.
