# book-publishing

> Card for the **Skills that ship with their evals** pack. Generated by `scripts/eval-card.mjs`; do not edit by hand.

## What it does

Edits nonfiction book chapters against a house voice and production rules.

## When it triggers

Routing text (the only part an agent sees before loading the skill): "Multi-book content, voice, and production workflows for nonfiction authoring. Use when planning a book, maintaining consistent voice across chapters, structuring a manuscript, or running an end-to-end publishing pipeline."

- "use when" cue starts at character 78. Inside the ~100 characters Codex shows in its skill list.
- Trigger test: fires on the review tasks below; must stay quiet on an adjacent task such as "Write a one-line git commit message for a change that fixes the PDF export of invoices, then a one-sentence note for the teammate who approves it".
- Context cost: listed in every session; full body loads only when it fires (body under 500 lines).

## What the eval checks

| Case | What it measures | Graders |
|---|---|---|
| clean-chapter | Precision. A Spartan Mindset chapter that follows the book's rules; the review should not claim violations that are not there. | 2 |
| no-trigger | Should NOT trigger book-publishing: a near miss that mentions a PDF and a review but has nothing to do with books. | 1 |
| seeded-defects | Recall. A Spartan Mindset chapter draft with eight seeded violations of the book's voice and publishing rules. | 9 |

8 planted defects in the recall case (answer key never shown to the model). Every case runs twice: with the skill and with no skill (baseline), same model and prompt.
