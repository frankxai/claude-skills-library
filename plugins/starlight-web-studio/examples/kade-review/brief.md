# Brief: Kade floating studios

**Kade is fictional**, invented for this worked review so nothing here imitates a real business.

- **Buyer.** A design-literate professional in Amsterdam, 35 to 55, who already owns or is buying a mooring right and is choosing between a conventional houseboat refit and a new build. They read specs, distrust "sustainable" as a word, and will pay for craft they can verify.
- **Job.** Decide in one visit whether Kade is serious enough to book a viewing.
- **One action.** Book a viewing in Amsterdam.
- **Facts the page may use** (from the spec sheet; nothing else may be claimed): 38 m² plus 9 m² loft; concrete hull 14 × 4.2 m; FSC larch; triple glazing; air-water heat pump; 9 kWp solar; 30 kWh LFP battery; seven-month build in Zaandam; from €248,000, mooring excluded.
- **References to beat.** A Dutch architecture practice's project page (plan-first, quiet type); a premium e-bike configurator (price and spec stated plainly); a boutique hotel's booking page (one action, no noise).
