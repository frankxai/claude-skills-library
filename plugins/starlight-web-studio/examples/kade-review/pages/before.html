The studio

Every Kade studio is built by hand in our Zaandam yard from FSC larch over a concrete hull, with triple glazing, a heat pump, a 9 kWp solar roof and a 30 kWh battery that keeps the lights on through a grey Dutch week. Inside you get one open room, a kitchen wall, a wet room and a sleeping loft, finished in oiled oak and lime plaster.
