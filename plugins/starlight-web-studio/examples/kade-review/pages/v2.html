A solar studio you can moor in a city canal.

One room, built to last on water

Each studio is built by hand in our Zaandam yard over seven months: FSC larch over a concrete hull, triple glazing and a heat pump.

Inside is one open room, a kitchen wall, a wet room and a sleeping loft. The roof carries 9 kWp of solar; the battery holds about four grey days.

How it works

  1. Secure a mooring

    Buy or lease a mooring right. We help with the permit file; the mooring is yours, not ours.

  2. Fix the plan

    Choose the loft, the kitchen wall and the finish. The price is fixed when the plan is.

  3. Launch and tow

    We launch in Zaandam and tow the studio to your mooring, then connect shore power and water.

Does the price include a mooring?

No. We help with the permit application, but the mooring right is bought or leased separately from the municipality or a private owner.

Can it run without a grid connection?

From April to September, yes, on a typical Dutch roof yield. In winter the battery covers about four grey days; after that it draws from shore power.
