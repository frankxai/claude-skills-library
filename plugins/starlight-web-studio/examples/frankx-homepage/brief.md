# Brief: frankx.ai homepage

Studio strategist, 2026-10-20. Inferred items are marked.

## Buyer

A technical founder or senior engineer who was just handed "the AI work" and wants a system they own, not another subscription. (Inferred from the FrankX brand promise, the AI Architect Academy buyer statement, and the site's own "built with your own keys" line. No demand data: the waitlist store is not provisioned.)

## The job

Decide in one visit whether Frank's blueprints are worth an afternoon: see one working example, take one free thing, leave with a next step.

## What they use today

Vendor docs, a few trusted builders' blogs, and X threads. Cost to them: weeks of piecing together patterns nobody has tested end to end.

## Three references to beat (opened 2026-10-20)

| Reference | What it does on the first screen | Beat it on |
|---|---|---|
| simonwillison.net | A dated stream of notes; the volume and dates are the proof. | **Proof density**: one inspectable artifact on the first screen instead of a list. |
| leerob.com | One line of identity ("Developer and writer."), no hero theatre. | **Clarity of offer**: say what the visitor can take today, not only who Frank is. |
| rauno.me | Craft carried by type and interaction, very little copy. | **Mobile feel**: Rauno's site disables zoom (`maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=no`); ours keeps it and still feels native. |

## The bar, in one line

Beats Simon Willison's weblog on proof density by putting one working blueprint, with its trace, in the first screen.

## Do not build

- A tour of every room in the estate on the homepage (today: about 30 phone screens).
- Book covers by other authors as decoration.
- A rotating headline.
