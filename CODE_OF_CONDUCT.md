# Code of conduct

We want people with different backgrounds and levels of experience to be able to learn and contribute here.

Be respectful and specific when giving feedback. Critique ideas and behavior without attacking people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, reveal private information, or post spam. Get consent before sharing another person's work, prompt, output, or story. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests, and discussions.

If something makes participation unsafe, email [frank@frankx.ai](mailto:frank@frankx.ai?subject=Community%20conduct%20report) with a link or description. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. The maintainer may remove content, close threads, or restrict participation when needed. We will handle reports with care and share them only as needed to respond; we cannot promise confidentiality beyond the systems used to receive them.

For a software vulnerability, use the [security policy](SECURITY.md) instead.
